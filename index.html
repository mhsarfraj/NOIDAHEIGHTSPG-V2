FAQ

Questions we hear often

For current availability and final terms, contact the property directly.

How far is the PG from NSEZ Metro Station?

The property is listed at about 400 metres, roughly a five-minute walk. Check the route on the map before you travel.

How do I confirm that a room is free?

Send the enquiry form, call 8799731416 or message us on WhatsApp. Availability changes, so get a confirmation before you visit or pay.

Is there a lock-in period?

No. There is no lock-in, and the notice period is 30 days.

Are meals included in the rent?

Meals are optional at ₹3,000 a month for breakfast and dinner, with Punjabi, South Indian and other menus.

Is there power backup or parking?

There is no power backup. Two-wheeler parking is available.

Can friends or family visit?

Visitors and guardians are allowed. Guests of the opposite gender are not allowed.

How do residents report a repair?

Sign in to the Resident Portal and send a maintenance request with the category, priority and details. You can track its status there.

How do I get my resident ID and login?

The owner creates your admission record. A unique resident ID is generated when it is saved and becomes your login.
